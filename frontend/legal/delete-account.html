Delete your CryptoDock account

CryptoDock (crypto.marketdock.in) · Last updated: 19 October 2026

Option 1 — delete it yourself in the app (fastest)

  1. Open CryptoDock (the Android app or crypto.marketdock.in) and sign in.
  2. Open Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2 — ask us by email

If you cannot sign in, email amitkmrai21@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "CryptoDock account deletion". We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your login (email address, name and sign-in details).
  • Your paper-trading portfolio saved on our server: virtual funds, positions and orders.
  • Data stored on your device (watchlist, price alerts, settings) is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

What we keep, and for how long

  • A record that your email address has already used the 7-day free trial, so the trial cannot be repeated. It contains no trading data.
  • Payment records (plan, amount, date and payment or purchase ID), only as long as accounting and tax law require.
  • Standard server request logs are removed automatically after a short time.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → CryptoDock.
