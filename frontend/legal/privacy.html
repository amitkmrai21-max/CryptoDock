Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

CryptoDock is a paper-trading and market-learning app. It never places real orders, never holds your money or crypto, and never asks for exchange or wallet keys.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (and name, if you sign in with Google) to create your account and keep your trial and plan. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, Supabase.
  • Plan and trial: when your free trial started, the plan you bought and until when it is valid.
  • Payments: on the website, payments are processed by Razorpay; in the Android app, subscriptions are billed by Google Play. We receive the order or purchase ID, plan, amount and status (for Google Play, a purchase token that we check with Google) — we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Paper-trading portfolio: your virtual funds, open positions and orders are saved on our server against your email, so the same portfolio shows on every phone and browser you sign in on. It is virtual money only.
  • On your device only: your watchlist, price alerts and app settings are stored in your browser/app storage on your device, not on our servers.
  • Technical logs: our server keeps standard request logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security and troubleshooting, kept for a limited time.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal data.
  • We don't show third-party advertising that tracks you.
  • We don't connect to your exchange account or place trades for you.

Services we use

  • Supabase — sign-in and account management.
  • Razorpay — plan payments on the website.
  • Google Play Billing — subscription payments inside the Android app.
  • Public market data feeds — live coin prices, charts and order books (no personal data is sent).
  • CoinGecko / ExchangeRate-API — the USDT → INR rate (no personal data is sent).
  • TradingView — chart widgets; it receives market data, not your account details.
  • News publishers — headlines and pictures come from their public feeds, and stories open on their own websites. Google Translate is used only when you tap "हिंदी में पढ़ें".

Notifications

If you allow notifications, price alerts you set are shown by your device. You can turn them off any time in your device or browser settings.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your login and the paper-trading portfolio saved on our server. Data on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. To keep the 7-day free trial fair, we keep a record that your email has already used its trial. To ask for any remaining data to be deleted, email us. Full steps: crypto.marketdock.in/delete-account.

Children

CryptoDock is meant for users aged 18 and above.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
