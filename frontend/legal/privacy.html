Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

CryptoDock is a paper-trading and market-learning app. It never places real orders, never holds your money or crypto, and never asks for exchange or wallet keys.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (and name, if you sign in with Google) to create your account and keep your trial and plan. Sign-in is handled by our authentication provider, Supabase.
  • Plan and trial: when your free trial started, the plan you bought and until when it is valid.
  • Payments: payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive the order and payment IDs, amount and status — we never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • On your device only: your paper-trading portfolio, orders, watchlist, price alerts and app settings are stored in your browser/app storage on your device, not on our servers.
  • Technical logs: our server keeps standard request logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security and troubleshooting, kept for a limited time.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal data.
  • We don't show third-party advertising that tracks you.
  • We don't connect to your exchange account or place trades for you.

Services we use

  • Supabase — sign-in and account management.
  • Razorpay — subscription payments.
  • Binance public market data — live coin prices, charts and order books (no personal data is sent).
  • CoinGecko / ExchangeRate-API — the USDT → INR rate (no personal data is sent).
  • TradingView and AI providers (Google Gemini, Groq) — only for features you open yourself; they receive market data, not your account details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, price alerts you set are shown by your device. You can turn them off any time in your device or browser settings.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account any time in the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your login. Paper-trading data on your device is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. To keep the 7-day free trial fair, we keep a record that your email has already used its trial. To ask for any remaining data to be deleted, email us.

Children

CryptoDock is meant for users aged 18 and above.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
